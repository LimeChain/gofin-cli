# Several edits under one original owner

A native batch may amend several members of one earlier batch. Fin keeps the original preview and submission unchanged and leaves every fill with that original operation. Each edit binds its native tag and original submission through protected journal context. Reusable client labels alone do not prove ownership.

The protected adapter prepares a full-original one-member revision for each tag. Its internal before/after binding replacements address existing, disjoint native fields; these paths are never exposed as caller passthrough. The broker independently checks the exact protected preimage and derives the whole-command accounting envelope. Positive principal, position, debt and exposure increases sum across members. A reduction in one member cannot finance an increase in another. Every child declares the same global fee and settlement ceilings; their increase is reserved once, while batches over distinct original owners retain separate sums.

An accepted edit or cancellation requires correlated native amendment audit and independent native order readback bound to the exact approved replacements. A transport acknowledgement is insufficient. Rejected outcomes need explicit native evidence; missing or ambiguous outcomes remain pending or unknown without replay. The original operation cannot reconcile through its own uncertain mutation; proved owners may recover while other owners or new financial orders remain pending. Cancellation children grant no new cash, position, debt, fee or USD authority. An accepted cancellation cannot activate the shared fee/PnL increases of an unknown amendment, and the original reservation remains until native fill/cancellation reconciliation.

SQLite schema 13 persists declared member links before dispatch; schema 14 adds declarations for additional grouped owners without rewriting earlier records. One transaction can combine several grouped owners, independently owned mutations and new financial receipts. Each new accepted subset commits an immutable cumulative revision and its original reservation atomically with member outcomes and proof events. Accepted principal transfers into the original reservation; unknown siblings retain their incremental reservation. A proof conflict or changed original record/reservation rolls back every part. The original monetary receipts retain their own immutable proof identities throughout restart and partial outcomes.

Grouped authority changes require fresh exact approval. Financial plans and operation-boundary consent cannot authorize them. Legacy edits that create several successor identities still require a separate reviewed lineage contract; the initial grouped seam supports unchanged native order identities. The generic tests establish synthetic engine/store behavior. Native adapter coverage, Touch ID and live-write evidence are tracked separately in the capability and evidence matrices.

Reproduce generic checks:

```sh
cargo test -p fin-domain grouped_amendments --lib
cargo test -p fin-engine grouped_ --lib
cargo test -p fin-store grouped_amendments --lib
cargo clippy -p fin-domain -p fin-store -p fin-engine --tests -- -D warnings
```
