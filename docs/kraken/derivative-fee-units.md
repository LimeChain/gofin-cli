# Derivative fee units, rebates and native currency deficits

Same-collateral derivative trading supports signed commissions. Current authenticated TradeVolume rates are admitted at the worst nonnegative maker/taker rate; a maker rebate cannot create spending headroom. Positive paid commission and negative-fee usable credit remain separate from realized PnL and funding. The original exact approval must include the credit identity. Old pending scopes without that authority remain unknown rather than receiving retroactive credit permission.

Every execution needs a unique matching PositionUpdate and AccountLog cash booking, its original market/wallet identity, a distinct immutable booking UID, exact signed fee/PnL/funding agreement, and `new_balance - old_balance = realized_pnl + realized_funding - fee`. Both cash balances must be nonnegative before that booking can establish usable credit. Execution proof events retain the raw native bodies across duplicate status and SQLite restart. `marketShareRebateCredited` has no denomination field and never creates an additional credit by itself. Only the specifically documented USD/`usd` cash spelling and the exact registered instrument's lowercase AccountLog spelling are normalized; unrelated currencies or contracts fail correlation.

Kraken publishes negative maker rates. Multi-M fees ordinarily debit USD, while non-USD collateral can be converted when USD is unavailable; FEE credits take precedence for trading fees. [Current derivative fee rules](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360048917612-fee-schedule), [maker rebate schedule](https://support.kraken.com/articles/futures-maker-rebate-april-2026).

Financial reconciliation explicitly requests `conversion_details: true`; the venue default omits these details. The pinned AccountLog schema exposes optional USD exchange rate, source currency, conversion-fee percentage and spread. These fields do not document a complete conversion group with exact source/destination principal, actual native fees and execution attribution. Fin does not turn an index valuation, haircut or rate into a fabricated debit/conversion proof. Converted fee settlement remains **incomplete**. A selected wallet with insufficient usable USD cannot silently spend another collateral asset. A positive native FEE-credit balance fails preparation with `KRAKEN_PREFERRED_FEE_CREDIT_UNIT_UNSUPPORTED` until its separately denominated debit is implemented. [AccountLog contract](https://docs.kraken.com/openapi/futures-history-rest.yaml).

Native negative `flex.currencies.USD.quantity` is observable as exact anchored `VariableDebt` under the actual protected AuthCheck account UID. It creates no positive holding. A duplicate native cash view fails observation; other negative currency meanings remain pending coverage. New trading requires usable USD, and transfers into a negative destination cannot claim plain usable cash credit. Execution bookings crossing a deficit stay pending because liability repayment and any remaining usable cash need separate effects. These restrictions apply to those account combinations, rather than all Derivatives accounts. [Accounts contract](https://docs.kraken.com/openapi/futures-rest.yaml).
