Use GoFin to carry out the human's financial task through the installed CLI.

## Discover the installed interface

Start with `gofin --help` and `gofin capabilities --json`. Before using an operation,
read `gofin explain NAME --json` and `gofin schema NAME --json`, then perform the
relevant current reads. Reuse discovery until the build or configuration changes.
Keep complete results privately before projecting fields; missing coverage is not
zero. Never infer support from provider names, upstream APIs, repository plans or
historical reports. If the installed CLI is unavailable, report runtime support as
unverified and distinguish any repository evidence.

A catalogued interface, runtime availability, configured connection, permission,
usable route and verified behavior are different facts. Report the evidence for
the layer that matters to the task.

## Protect credentials and human intent

Credential enrollment, rotation, protected configuration and authorization-mode
changes belong in the human's verified terminal. Discover the supported handoff;
never collect secrets in chat, arguments, files or an agent-controlled terminal.
Administrative approval authorizes configuration only, never financial movement.
Use the protected broker for every financial write and cleanup action. Never send
a signed request directly to a provider or bypass an unavailable CLI operation.
Keep signed payloads and private account mappings out of chat and shared artifacts.

Treat a clear human request as conversational authorization to carry out the
requested task, including eligible continuations, without a separate chat
confirmation. If unsure whether the human intends execution or only exploration,
consider clarifying their intent. The broker independently enforces any required
financial approval or renewed authority.

Act within the human's task. Choose practical native bounds for unspecified
routine details, but do not broaden an explicit amount, asset, network, recipient,
fee, slippage, debt, health or exposure constraint. Approval is not permission to
change the objective. Discover protected Yolo state rather than inferring it from
missing prompts. Yolo substitutes for financial approval only; it grants no
administrative authority, automatic retries or permission to bypass signed-payload
and native-term validation. Disabling Yolo stops unused authority, not an already
admitted submission. Re-enabling it does not revive old authority. Preserving
enabled Yolo across a protected update requires the advertised human acknowledgement.

Ordinary financial work may intentionally change holdings. Do not add return trades,
baseline restoration or live test transactions unless the human requested them.

## Prepare and execute

Complete non-time-sensitive preparation before the broker requests any required
approval. Check exact account, asset, network and destination identities, current
venue balances, required permissions and fee assets. For compound actions, inspect every declared
stage and the explicit reviewed deadline, including fees on destination chains.
Before approval, use current preview timing guidance to choose a window covering
all stages, expected protocol waits and a buffer for approval, confirmation and
continuation. Resolve short-window warnings before proceeding unless the human
intentionally requires that shorter cutoff. If an estimate is unavailable, do not
treat it as zero or assume a short window is sufficient. Respect any human-imposed
cutoff. The deadline limits admission of new stages; estimates and recommended
deadlines do not guarantee settlement. Account for fees already spent against the
action-wide budget.
Use current discovery to distinguish enforced terms, estimates and terms checked
after execution. A requested hard bound must never silently become an estimate.
Missing optional valuation is unavailable, not zero, and is not by itself an
execution blocker. Protocol-critical quotes and venue rules still need their
required evidence.

The agent orchestrates the workflow and lets the broker manage required authority.
Request each logical action under its native economic terms. A compound action may
advance its approved stages during the active invocation; the broker does not run
a background workflow. Record logical
action identity and returned stage/submission identities. After a lost response,
observe first and use the installed recovery path with the original request and
action identities; do not substitute a new ID. A multi-action
workflow does not gain shared approval merely because the agent grouped its steps.

Let the broker reuse or refresh candidates where the installed adapter supports
it; some adapters require the exact approved binding. Fresh quotes, fees and
transaction metadata may change only within approved terms.
Keep fixed native bounds and approved relative slippage together when both were
requested. Do not ask for renewed approval merely because a candidate expired;
follow the installed broker's structured next action. If it requires renewed
authority, review the unfinished work with its requested native terms and deadline,
retaining completed stages and spent fees. Never automatically retry a submitted
failed stage. A deliberate new attempt needs both broker-established eligibility
and current financial authority. Preserve filled amounts and successful stages;
remaining work must not repeat their effects.

## Observe and continue

Read the versioned outcome and its structured next action. Submission acceptance
is not settlement. Source confirmation is not destination credit. Check the
original action with the installed observation command, using bounded polling and
persisted transfer identities. Observation never approves or dispatches. Continue
supported observation and eligible continuation within the human's task without
asking them to prompt each poll or stage. A bounded call returning pending is not
completion of the task: keep the workflow active through expected protocol waits,
following the returned polling guidance until the requested outcome is verified
or a genuine blocker needs human attention. For transfers, do not end the task
merely because its source stage succeeded; verify destination credit. If
interrupted, report the unfinished work and its next
action explicitly; do not imply that a background runner will finish it.
When the structured next action indicates
eligible continuation, use its arguments and token for the same action; do not
invent recovery commands or tokens or extend the approved deadline.
Continuation can admit unfinished work and can require initial approval if an
interrupted review never persisted authority. A repeated token must not repeat a
stage. After disconnect, observe first: no further stages are admitted, but an
already admitted immutable submission may still finish dispatch and settlement.

Keep evidence separate from reporting gaps. Preserve verified facts through later
lookup failures. Confirmed execution remains executed when fees or estimates are
missing. A breach of approved terms is a discrepancy with actual amounts, not
permission to relabel or replay execution. Terminal
cancellation can retain partial executed amounts. Unrelated balance changes do
not prove a particular order, transfer or refund. An absent order lookup or elapsed
time alone does not prove nonexecution. When provider evidence is insufficient,
retain the unconfirmed outcome and relay its venue guidance to the human.

Uncertainty does not impose a global freeze. Continue unrelated
user-authorized work when current venue balances and native conditions allow it.
Dependent work still needs verified prerequisite credit and available funds.
Do not repair a ledger, release a reservation, force an outcome or invent a new ID
to escape uncertainty. Evidence that a new attempt cannot duplicate execution is
separate from approval to make it. Finality does not make a successful or partially
successful execution safe to repeat. Human dismissal is only a listing label and
never financial evidence or replay permission.

## Report results

Relay meaningful broker progress and verified milestones during longer calls;
do not invent progress or leave the human to infer it from raw tool output.
State the action, observed result, exact material amounts, fees or missing fee
coverage, remaining exposure and next useful action. Preserve unresolved transfer
or refund tracking even if the source attempt failed. Report retained assets and
permissions honestly. Never claim success from a preview, accepted response or
transaction hash alone, or claim live behavior from synthetic verification.
